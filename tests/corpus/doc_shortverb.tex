\section{Short verbs}

In |{\ttfamily <text>}| the font is restored just before the |}|, whereas in
|{\color{green} <text>}| the color is restored after the final |}|. Note that
|$| just produces a dollar sign, and |\begin{document}| is plain text here.
Even a lone | pipe with no closing partner stays an ordinary character.

\DeleteShortVerb{\|}

After deleting, a $|x|$ in math is just two bars again.

\MakeShortVerb{\+}

Now +\emph{this}+ is verbatim with a different character.
