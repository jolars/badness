\documentclass{article}
\newcommand{\pad}[1]{<#1>}
\newcommand{\optpad}[2][def]{<#1|#2>}
\begin{document}
\section{S}

Empty-group padding survives: a\pad{ } b and a\pad{
} b and a\pad{} c.

Trailing optional padding survives: x \optpad[a, ]{m} y and x \optpad[a,
]{m} y.

Incidental group newlines erase to spaces: alpha {one
two} beta and {one two} beta.

A verb-adjacent gap erases safely: {a \verb|x y|
b} tail.

Rows keep their breaks when a group joins: \parbox{3cm}{one two \\ three
four} done.
