% A visible non-verbatim redefinition of a built-in braced-verbatim command
% (\code, jss) shadows the built-in: \code{...} then lexes as an ordinary
% group the formatter is free to lay out, not an opaque VERB (issue #53
% follow-up). Cross-file redefinitions stay a tolerated false negative.
\newcommand{\code}[1]{\ensuremath{\mathsf{#1}}}

The sequence \code{code} is now an ordinary macro call, so its argument is a
real group and the formatter may reflow the surrounding text just like any
other command with a mandatory argument, wrapping across the line as needed.

A redefined \url also lexes normally:
\renewcommand{\url}[1]{\texttt{#1}}
See \url{example} for details.
